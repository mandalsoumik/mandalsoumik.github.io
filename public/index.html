Soumik Mandal

I am a Research Scientist in Population Health at the NYU Grossman School of Medicine, working at the intersection of digital health, human–AI interaction, and engagement phenotyping. My research examines how AI-enabled and data-driven technologies are reshaping care delivery, patient–provider communication, and clinicians’ well-being alongside the broader social dynamics of healthcare shaped by trust, expectation, workload, and health equity.

My recent projects focus on:

  • User engagement with digital interventions for chronic disease management: investigating what engagement means for patients, causal effects on engagement on health outcomes, and how behavioral phenotyping can enable adaptive, personalized, and equitable intervention design.
  • Impacts of Generative AI supported care delivery on patient advice seeking: evaluating how AI-mediated messaging and remote triage influence clinical workload and patients' perceptions of care quality, clinician competence, and trust.

My work is supported by the National Institutes of Health (NIH) and the Agency for Healthcare Research and Quality (AHRQ).
